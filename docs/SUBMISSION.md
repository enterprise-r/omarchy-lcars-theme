I'd like to share **LCARS for Omarchy — a quiet view at warp**.

It combines classic peach/lavender LCARS panels with a live STARDATE clock and
an animated starfield behind the entire bar. Stars travel outward from behind
the clock, growing into white-blue trails. It is designed for everyday coding,
with restrained transparency and working native system controls.

Repo: https://github.com/yzingher/omarchy-lcars-theme

The complete experience targets Omarchy 4/Quattro's Quickshell shell (tested on
4.0.0.alpha). One installer sets up the theme and widgets together, preserves
custom widgets, makes backups, and supports uninstall. Palette-only,
no-animation, and opaque options are available. The README includes a real
desktop screenshot and animated bar preview.

Would this fit the Extra Themes page, with a note that the full bar requires
Quickshell and the repository's installer?
